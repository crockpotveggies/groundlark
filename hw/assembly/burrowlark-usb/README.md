# Burrowlark SHT45 assembly identity

U6 is Sensirion SHT45-AD1B-R2, JLCPCB C5221601, DFN-4 1.5 x 1.5 mm.
See `sht45-selection.json` for manufacturer evidence, numbered pin mapping and
dated catalog observations. Stock is not reserved and must be checked at order
time. C10 is the local 100 nF bypass on the switched sensor rail.

Keep the sensing opening free of flux, conformal coating and potting. Supplier
pad/rotation review and physical assembly qualification remain required before
fabrication approval. This selection evidence does not publish a release package.
